BoG

How to play

The goal

Each player has three strongholds: a left front, a right front, and a back where the leader stands. Each stronghold holds a champion. A champion's HP is how much damage it can take, and when it reaches 0, its stronghold falls. Knock down the enemy's back stronghold to win.

Setting up

Pick three champions from the ones you are offered. Put two at the front and your leader at the back. A leader's ability only works from the back.

Your turn

  • You get 2 action points at the start of your turn. From the third turn of the player who goes first, you get 3 every turn.
  • Each champion can use its ability once per turn, and each ability costs 1 action point.
  • Playing a utility card costs 1 action point.
  • Unused action points are lost when you press End turn.
  • You draw one utility card at the start of each turn, up to 7 in your hand. The player who goes first doesn't draw on their first turn.

Attacking

  • Champions attack enemy champions with their abilities. A champion at the back can still attack.
  • Enemy fronts can always be attacked.
  • The enemy's back can't be attacked until one of their fronts has fallen.

Words on the cards

  • Shield blocks damage before it reaches HP. A champion can hold up to 6.
  • Dazed means the champion can't act on its owner's next turn.
  • Bleed means the champion loses HP at the start of its owner's turn, for 3 turns.
  • Marked means the next hit on the champion deals double damage.
  • Swift gives a 10% chance to dodge each attack, for each stack, up to 3.
  • Linked comes from Soul Link. The linked champion takes damage at the start of each of your turns.
  • Boosted means the champion deals extra damage from Tailwind or Gust, for the rest of this turn and your next turn.

Sudden death

Games can't go on forever. From turn 21, your champions take damage at the start of each of your turns, and the damage grows every 10 turns.

Champions

Each champion's abilities, passive, and leader ability are listed under its card. A leader's ability only works from the back.

Utility cards

Each one costs 1 action point to play.
